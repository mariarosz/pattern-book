# Review, 25 November 2026

Five reviewer agents (definitions in `.claude/agents/`) looked at the project before its first public version. This is a record of what they found and what was done. The final decisions were Maria's.

## Per entry: motion craft, prompt and provenance

All eight entries were reviewed from 12-frame strips and their source.

Fixed
- Patchwork: pattern grounds used colours outside the palette. Rebuilt from palette colours at low opacity; the check script now rejects any other colour.
- Running stitch: the loop jumped at the seam (half a turn instead of a full one), and the dash period did not divide the circumference, leaving a doubled stitch.
- Gauze: the palest threads disappeared at small sizes.
- Loose thread: text promised six loops; the curve shows five.
- Button: the two stitches left together where the prompt asked for one after the other; the rim was thinner than the spec allows.
- Warp field: the lean pivoted around the page corner instead of the top of the threads.

Found and not yet fixed
- Gauze: the over/under gaps drift slightly while a thread is still sliding in.
- Nothing has been tested in Safari.
- Every `prompt.md` for a multi-turn entry is a consolidated prompt. None has been run cold yet, which is why every entry says `"reproduced": false`.

## Whole site: art direction, front end, first 60 seconds

Fixed
- Header collided on phones; caption rows were ragged.
- Reduced motion: tiles now show the poster and no play control.
- Pause buttons had no accessible name beyond "Pause".
- Muted text failed AA contrast (3.95:1); now 5.3:1.
- The check script could be bypassed with single-quoted or spaced attributes; it now scans the whole file.
- Default 404 page; route prefetching on every tile.
- The site said "the prompts that made them". Until the prompts are run cold it says "the prompts behind them".

Fixed in a second pass
- Twill floats halved in thickness; Patchwork stitched in linen instead of ink; no loop passes through an empty square any more.
- Category filters appear only once a category holds more than one entry.

Open
- Four of six categories hold a single entry.
